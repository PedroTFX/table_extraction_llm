The taxonomic identification of the INSECT in the record — the name of the insect taxon (species, genus, or higher) as it appeared in the source.

This column says WHICH INSECT each row's measurements belong to — the FOCAL organism the record is about. It must be an insect taxon, never a place, group, condition, or a non-insect organism (e.g. plants).

A column naming an ASSOCIATED organism the focal insect interacts with — its host, prey, host/food plant, or symbiont (a "Host spider(s)", "Host plant", "Prey" column) — is NOT verbatimIdentification even when its values are taxon names. That is a measurementType (a biotic-association trait) whose value is the associated organism. Only the studied insect is verbatimIdentification.

Map a column here ONLY if its values are insect taxon names — usually a Latin binomial/genus, optionally with qualifiers (sp., cf., ?, authorship).
Valid: Linepithema humile, Camponotus cruentatus, Peromyscus sp.

DO NOT map these:
Non-insect organisms, especially PLANT species (e.g. "Quercus robur", flower/host-plant names) -> not this.
Sites/localities/places (e.g. "Portbou (Girona)") -> locality fields, not this.
Analysis or functional groups (e.g. "Group 2", "hot climate specialists").
Treatments/habitats/vegetation (e.g. "Pasture", "Grassland").
Statistic or variable names (e.g. "Estimate ± SE", "FDis", "Worker size").

Rule of thumb: "is this the NAME OF AN INSECT?" If it's a plant, place, group, treatment, or statistic, it is NOT verbatimIdentification — and the table may have no specimen identity at all.
