Definition: Any treatment applied to samples PRIOR to analysis or storage (e.g., preservation, drying, staining).

Notes: the key word here is prior/before, if the observation was done and then it was collected that means that the treatment was null

Examples:
- "preserved in ethanol"
- "air-dried"
- "fixed in formalin"

Guidance: Prefer concise descriptions; include concentrations or durations when reported.
