Definition: The ontogenetic stage (developmental phase) of the biological individual(s) at the time of recording.

Valid values (insect-relevant examples):
- egg
- larva
- caterpillar (Lepidoptera-specific larva)
- nymph (hemimetabolous insects like termites, grasshoppers)
- instar I, instar II, etc. (numbered larval stages)
- pupa
- adult
- unknown — when not specified

Notes:
- For social insects, "soldier" and "worker" are CASTES, not life stages. Soldiers and workers are usually adult forms.
- If the paper studies adults of social insects (most studies do), use "adult".
- If life stage is not specified, default to "adult" for adult-only studies (e.g., morphometrics on collected workers/soldiers) or "unknown" if truly ambiguous.

Examples: egg, larva, nymph, instar I, pupa, adult, unknown
