The place where SAMPLING or OBSERVATION happened, exactly as written in the source — a property of the sampling event, not of the organism. Use it only for the geographic location of the study/collection.

Use verbatimLocality when a column's values are the place sampling occurred:
Countries or regions: "Germany", "Nouvelle-Aquitaine", "Western Australia"
Named study sites, reserves, plots, or site codes: "Portbou (Girona)", "Charles Darwin National Park", "AG", "HF"
The landscape/land-use where sampling took place, used as the sampling location: "oilseed crops", "herbaceous adjacent habitat" (when it labels WHERE a sample was taken)

Do NOT use verbatimLocality for a biological trait that happens to reference a place. If the column describes something ABOUT the organism — where it lives, nests, feeds, or its ecological/habitat classification — that is a property of the organism and belongs in measurementType, NOT verbatimLocality.

Examples
Countries or regions: "Germany", "Nouvelle-Aquitaine", "Western Australia"
Named sites, reserves, or study areas: "Portbou (Girona)", "Charles Darwin National Park", site codes like "AG", "HF"
Habitat / vegetation / land-use descriptors used as the location of sampling: "Grassland", "Savanna woodland", "oilseed crops", "herbaceous adjacent habitat"
Any free-text place string given in the source

Guidance: Preserve original wording and punctuation; do not attempt to geocode here. 
If locality needs normalization, record separate normalized fields.
