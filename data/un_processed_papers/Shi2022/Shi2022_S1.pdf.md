Appendix 3. Redundancy analysis (RDA) of wild bee communities, constrained by the amount of semi-natural habitat (SNH) and field size (FS). RDA axis 1 and 2 explained 15.5% and 4.8% of the distribution, respectively. The adjusted $\mathtt { R } ^ { 2 }$ was 0.08. The full model is significant $F = 1.73, p = 0.021$ , F distribution based on 999 permutations). Variation in pollinator community composition was captured by the first axis (F= 2.65, P=0.02), while responses on the second axis were non-significant $( \mathsf{F} = 0.8, \mathsf{P} = 0.695 )$ . Based on permutation test (n=999), the impact of SNH was significant $(p = 0.01)$ , while the effect of FS was not $(p = 0.451)$
