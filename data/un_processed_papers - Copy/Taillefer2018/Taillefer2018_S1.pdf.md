Type wetland: Restriction to a wetland habitat – R: restricted (found in only one wetland type), I: intermediate (found in two wetland types), B: broad (found in three wetland types)

Freq occurrence: Frequency of occurrence – 1: only found in one site, 2: found in 2 to 6 sites, 3: found in 7 to 13 sites, 4: found in 14 to 16 sites.

No specimens: Number of specimens collected – R: rare < 0.6% of total catch, C: common ≥ 0.6% of catch

Voltinism: U: univoltine, M: bivoltine/ multivoltine.

Overwin stage: Overwintering stage – E: eggs, L: larvae, P: pupae, A: adults.

Specializ: Specialization – O: obligate (specialist found exclusively in wetlands), A: amphibious (at least part of their life cycle in wetlands and remainder in terrestrial system), F: facultative (occasionally found in wetlands for water or food)
