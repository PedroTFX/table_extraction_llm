Why the prose scores stay low

The 3 dev papers read by the tree (t1): each GT row and each of our rows traced through the saved tree, facts and sort files, with no model and no scorer. Labels come from code rules, so read them as a guide and check the rows.

Verdict: mostly neither missing data nor bad questions

We find most of the GT. 1,121 of 1,363 GT rows match exactly (82%). The decision questions lose 2 rows and the reading misses about 24.

The score falls on our 925 rows with no GT partner. Most of the ones sampled look like true facts at a finer level than the GT: we write Pronotum, Elytra and Head pieces and the whole field too, and we keep altitudes the Xia2022 GT leaves out. That is an architecture and output choice. Even with every extra removed, judged F1 would reach only about 0.89.

Architecture costs more than questions on the misses too. About 113 misses are structural: repeated records we write too few times, and habitat or group facts printed once but owed to several species. These are the ones to fix. There is real junk among the extras as well, such as 'habitat = Bi leg.' and rows for species only named in comparisons. It has not been counted by hand.
