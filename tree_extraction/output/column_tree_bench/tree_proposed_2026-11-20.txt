  table_kind: What does each row of this table hold?                                                    [UNCHANGED]
    - organism_traits: One organism per row: a species or another taxon (a genus, a morphospecies). The other columns either name groups the organism belongs to (its family, its order) or give its characteristics (its measurements, its categories, what it does or where it lives).
    - specimens: One individual per row: a single collected, observed or measured individual, with the values recorded on it. Several rows can belong to the same species.
    - long_format: One characteristic per row (a long layout): one column names the characteristic (its cells read, for example, 'frond length', 'leaf width') and other columns give its value; the organism repeats over several rows, or is named in the caption or the header.
    - counts: Counts per row: how many individuals of an organism were found at a site, in a sample, on a date or under a treatment (abundance, occurrence, presence at sites).
    - statistics: A result of an analysis per row: the output of a statistical test or model (estimates, test statistics, p-values, correlations, information criteria), not values describing organisms.
    - other: Anything else: descriptions of sites or environments, definitions of codes or abbreviations, methods, or lists of names without values.
